What is known does not reliably become what people can do.

54.9%

of recommended care received by US adults, across 30 conditions.1

What this measures
Measures
Recommended care received, by condition. It ranged from 78.7% for cataract to 10.5% for alcohol dependence.
Does not show
Care in other countries, or care today. The study was published in 2003.

16.5%

of people with major depressive disorder, across 21 countries, received minimally adequate treatment.2

What this measures
Measures
Household surveys of 51,547 people in the WHO World Mental Health Surveys. About 1 in 5 received minimally adequate treatment in high-income countries, and 1 in 27 in low- and lower-middle-income countries.
Does not show
Countries outside the 21 surveyed, or change since the surveys were run.

~17 years

from research to practice: a figure several studies reached while measuring different stages of the process.3

What this measures
Measures
A review of 23 papers that quantified time lags in health research. Few were comparable, because they measured different things at different points.
Does not show
A single measured average. The authors caution that the agreement on 17 years hides important differences.
